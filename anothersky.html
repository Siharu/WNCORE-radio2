On March 31, 2028, Som was alive. On April 1, 2032, he was still 28.

He does not remember the four years between. He was not here for them. He was in a different world — one where Anne existed, where his mother was four years younger, where the sky was the right color. Now he is here. And the sky is wrong.

The outbreak has started in Nepal. The WHO has declared a pandemic. His mother mentions World War III the way people mention old weather. Nobody finds this strange. Som finds everything strange. He cannot explain why.

He has not yet checked the year.
