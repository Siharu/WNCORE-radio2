## 1. THE UNIVERSE STRUCTURE

There are two known worlds relevant to Another Sky:

### World-1 (The Original Earth)
The world Som came from. Mirrors our real world closely. Currently in **2028**. No WW3. No Blank Zone. No outbreak. Anne is alive here. Som's mother is alive and younger here.

### World-2 (Another Sky)
A parallel Earth where history diverged slightly after **1946** — small changes that compounded into massive ones. Currently in **2032**, four years ahead of World-1. Has already lived through WW3, the Blank Zone, and now the outbreak.

**The Sky:** Literally looks wrong. Sometimes too red, like it's bleeding. Wrong cloud formations. Wrong colors at sunset. Stars in incorrect positions on some nights. Visual glitches. This is not poetic — the sky in World-2 genuinely behaves differently.

### The Swap
On **March 31, 2028** (World-1 time), Som vanished.  
On **March 31, 2032** (World-2 time), Som appeared — confused, disoriented, with no memory of the four years in between because **he didn't live them.**

His body never aged. His hands are 2028 hands. His phone is a 2028 model. His clothes are 2028 style. He jumped four years in an instant without experiencing a single second of them. He doesn't know this yet when the story begins.

> *Who or what took his place in World-1 is unknown. Whether anyone noticed he was gone is unknown.*

## 2. MAIN CHARACTER — SOM

**Name:** Som  
**Age:** 28 (body is still 2028 — hasn't aged)  
**Born:** 2003  
**Nationality:** Bangladeshi  
**Location:** Dhaka, Bangladesh  

### Background
Bangladeshi civil engineer at a Japanese engineering firm in Dhaka. Specializes in structural design, load calculations, and CAD drawings. Wanted to pursue environmental engineering but gave up due to job insecurity. Gets scolded by his boss in the early chapters for missing dimensions and calculations.

### Personality (Pre-Outbreak)
- Confident at home, awkward and misunderstood at work
- Lies unconsciously — not maliciously, it's just a habit he can't fully explain
- Smirks when he's proven right
- Avoids arguments unless it's personal
- Jack-of-all-trades, master of none — he knows a lot about many things but not deeply enough to be an expert in any single one, because his mind never stays still long enough
- Tech-savvy gamer, anime enthusiast

### Personality (Post-Incident Zero)
- Becomes stoic and cold
- Highly indecisive but calculative
- Develops a singular, driving purpose

### Appearance
- Height: 5'11"
- Weight: 72 kg
- Build: Mesomorph but not muscular (doesn't work out)
- Greying hair — linked to past trauma
- Wears glasses

### Languages
- Fluent: Bangla, English
- Basic: Japanese (anime and work exposure)

### Likes
Gacha games, mystery and thriller anime, his mom, Anne's music and hair.

### His Situation in World-2
When Som arrives in World-2 he doesn't realize anything is wrong at first. He goes about daily life. Then his mother casually mentions WW3 — and his blood runs cold. Because there was no WW3 in the world he came from.

He starts noticing other things. Anne's photos are gone from his phone. He can't remember the faces of eight other people who were important to him. The world feels slightly off in ways he can't articulate.

He doesn't look at the year on his phone. Only the date. For weeks, he never looks at the year.

## 3. SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

### Som's Mother

**Age:** 68 (2028), 72 in World-2 (2032)  
**Religion:** Hindu  
**Background:** Ex-primary school teacher

**Survival history:**
- 1971 Bangladesh Independence War (shot with pellets)
- 1970 Bhola Cyclone (deadliest cyclone in history)
- Bangladesh famine (1974–75)
- Coup and civil war
- COVID-19
- WW3 (2025–2027)
- The Blank Zone (2028–2031)

Her husband left her and married someone else when Som was two months old. She raised Som alone.

**Physical condition:**
- Weak legs
- Paralyzed fingers
- Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD) and anger issues
- Constant muscle pain — needs Som to massage her legs every morning and before sleep
- Slight insomnia
- Owns two fridges; batch cooks meals due to limited mobility

**Relationship with Som:** Emotionally intense, deeply bonded, but doesn't fully trust him because of his unconscious lying habit. Would sometimes listen to strangers over him. A pattern in the family — they brush him off as young and dumb even though he's nearly 28. Som doesn't know why.

**Transformation — Incident Zero:**  
Occurs approximately two weeks after the outbreak starts. Som goes on a supply run, leaving his mother barricaded. She gets bitten by both a zombie and an infected simultaneously. She becomes the **first Ghuul in existence.**

She doesn't harm Som. She runs instead. But she destroys other survivor camps. She secretly watches Som from afar.

---

### Anne

**Nationality:** Filipina  
**Age:** ~25 in 2028 (would be ~29 in 2032 — but she doesn't exist in World-2)  
**Profession:** Artist, dancer, singer  
**Involvement:** Local political group — wants to climb the social structure to fix the corruption she's seen since childhood

**Relationship with Som:** Six-year long-distance relationship. Met online. Broke up with Som ~1–2 months before he was transferred — she wanted to focus on her goals but still loved him and hoped to be with him when things settled. Som didn't fully understand that part of her. They argued about it over messages. Anne wanted him to wait, build himself, so they could stand next to each other properly when the time was right.

**Status in World-2:** Never existed. No photos remain. No conversation logs. Her face is fading from Som's memory. She was the one whose face calmed him on his worst days.

She is the first of the Nine Missing People.

---

### The Nine Missing People

Eight others — 4 males, 3 females — who were important to Som in World-1 and have been completely erased from World-2. He can't remember their faces. He knows they existed. He gradually uncovers their erased existence through the course of survival.

### The Infection — What Is It?
Multiple competing in-world theories:
- Signal-based brain rewiring (not a virus at all)
- An undiscovered virus spreading silently
- A fungus hidden in the bloodstream
- A drug causing mass hallucinations — people aren't actually turning, they just *think* they see monsters
- Classic undead (hearts don't beat but bodies move)

A lone underground scientist claimed it was a **prion disease** before broadcasting that theory online and disappearing entirely. His lab and body were never found.

### Animals
- Dogs: unaffected
- Cats: die if bitten
- Cows: die
- Chimpanzees: can zombify but die within weeks
- Most mammals: same fate as chimps
- Insects: unaffected
- Plants: can get infected but are mostly harmless unless you eat the wood. Fruits appear unaffected but die off. Infected trees stop producing scent from their flowers.

### Internet
Still operational in pockets worldwide.

## 8. GHUULS

Created when a person is bitten by **both** a zombie and an infected simultaneously. The body almost always rejects this — only extremely strong-willed individuals survive the dual infection and fully turn. No living specimen has ever been captured.

**Appearance:** Pure white skin and hair. Hair occasionally turns brown when walking in the Rain of Obsedia — they seem to enjoy it, like they're briefly human again, playing in rain like a child. The moment the rain stops they return to destruction.

**Behavior:** Highly agile — survivors describe them as teleporting. Can wipe out an entire base in seconds without survivors understanding what happened. Shooting the brain does not stop them.

> *"What does stop it then?" — a Drifter*

**Known count:** 173 Ghuuls confirmed worldwide as of deep into the story. Updated continuously by WNCORE radio network. Every country has at least one.

**The First Ghuul — Som's Mother (Incident Zero):**  
Bitten by both types while Som was on a supply run. Becomes pure white. Eyes flicker between brown and black. Glitches between realities. Does not harm Som — runs instead. Destroys other survivor camps. Secretly watches Som from afar. Drives his entire quest forward.

## 9. WORLD ANOMALIES

### Rain of Obsedia (Black Rain)
Begins globally two weeks after the outbreak. Deep black rainfall. Also called: *Black Rain, Moon Rain, Starfall* depending on region.

Becomes a daily occurrence — people stop being afraid of it. Underground scientists report its chemical properties are identical to normal rain except for harmless acidic materials. Some believe it would have happened regardless of the outbreak — a cosmic or natural phenomenon with its own timeline.

When the black rain falls, the sky looks like it's bleeding. Everything goes dark like nighttime as the sky turns gooey and dark. Some observers report that infected become calm and still when the rain falls on them.

**Effect on Ghuuls:** They seem to enjoy it. Their hair briefly turns brown. They act almost human. Then the rain stops and they don't.

### Fog of Medusa
Yellowish fog that has only appeared in Germany so far. Kills and suffocates everything organic — including infected. Only zombies (fully undead) are unaffected. Its connection to events in other stories is unknown in-world.

### The Global Chilling
Begins two months into the outbreak. The planet is cooling. At least 5–6 hail events per month worldwide. When black rain and hail happen simultaneously it is the worst possible time to be outside.

### The Great Migration
Trillions of ants and other insects are moving north. Birds and wild animals are following. Nobody knows if they are running from something or toward something.

## 10. FACTIONS

### WNCORE
*"World News of Companion and Organized Radio Establishment"*  
A network of radio station people formed after the outbreak. Broadcasts worldwide news, Ghuul sighting updates, and survival information via radio. Tracks and updates the known Ghuul count. One of the only reliable global communication systems still operating.

---

### Major Factions

**The Remaining Governments**  
USA, UK, Canada, India, France, Germany, South Korea, Finland, and African governments have consolidated in an Alaska stronghold. They call themselves humanity's last hope. In reality they appear to be planning to nuke every zombie/infected zone and restart from scratch, protecting themselves first. Japan has its own remote island stronghold. Australia has gone completely dark. Russia built a massive steel border wall — most of the country is still zombie-territory with Ghuul sightings.

**The Moon Dwellers**  
High society elites — leaders, ministers, billionaires — who evacuated to the Moon Dome the moment the outbreak started. The cleaning company that built the dome appears to have had a prior arrangement with them. Nobody knows if they're alive. Theories range from: they're planning to terraform the Moon and wait for Earth to reset naturally, to: the cleaning company killed them on arrival and is using them as a food source.

**The Blood Pact**  
Formerly known as the Brotherhood Pact — a small-time biker gang based in Mexico. Now a powerful criminal syndicate operating out of Antarctica, dealing in organ smuggling. How they became this large this fast after the outbreak is unknown.

**Rooftop Seers**  
Formerly a religious radio station broadcasting God's news and advice before the outbreak. Now peaceful broadcasters. Secretly: they are infected.

---

### Minor / International Factions

**Pale Node** — Emergency signal broadcasters. Goals are ambiguous. Were a group of scientists who did Sunday podcasts.

**Kraken's Paw** — Fishermen and oil rig workers stranded in the southern Pacific who banded together post-outbreak. Not originally a group.

**White Flag** — A pacifist aid faction operating from an empty cruise ship near London. Originally an NGO distributing food to homeless people, based in Malaysia. Has influence far beyond what their size should allow. Their leader may be a billionaire.

**Logbook Drifters** *(unofficial name — they don't name themselves)*  
Unknown number of Drifters worldwide. They communicate with each other through unknown means and seem to have comprehensive situational awareness globally. They produce mass-distributed logbooks covering every zombie and infected type, their traits, country situations, and survivor intelligence. They leave these logbooks in survival bases, malls, supply locations.

Their logo: an owl holding down a lizard, ready to strike.

*Warning:* A counter-Drifter faction is actively planting fake logbooks with false information. Every logbook you find could be fabricated.

### Chapters 1–3 · March 25–31, 2032

**Chapter 1 — Static Sky**  
Som wakes disoriented at the office. Boss scolds him for dozing off. A billboard glitches — conspiracy footage flashes, then returns to normal. His mother calls to scold him. He finishes work at 9:37 PM. Stares at the moon. The moon shimmers. He checks his phone for the date — sees March 25. Doesn't look at the year.

*"The sky is warning him. And only him."*

**Chapter 2 — Office Party**  
A party for four new employees. Boss sings terribly. Som leaves early. The party devolves into something darker. By the chapter's end — Som is transferred.

**Chapter 3 — The Last Normal Day**  
Date: March 31. Small details feel wrong. The billboard glitches again. His mother mentions WW3 casually — Som's blood runs cold. He searches for Anne in his contacts. All images of her are gone. He searches for the other eight. He cannot remember their faces. He still hasn't looked at the year.

---

### Chapters 4–8 · April 1–8, 2032

**Chapter 4** — News of outbreak in Nepal villages. Mother is scared. Som reassures her.

**Chapter 5** — Outbreak spreads. WHO declares pandemic. Society panics. Som stocks supplies, barricades the house.

**Chapter 6** — Deaths confirmed. Society breaks down. Government abandons lower-income areas. Som makes his first dangerous supply run.

**Chapter 7 — THE REVELATION**  
Som picks up an official document. Sees the date. Looks more carefully. Sees the **year: 2032.**

> *"Wait. 2032? What the hell?"*

He looks at his hands — same as 2028. Checks a mirror — no aging, no scars. Grabs his phone — still a 2028 model. His hair is the same length it was four years ago.

> *"I didn't forget four years. I wasn't here for them. I jumped from 2028 to 2032. In an instant."*

Everything connects: his mother's age. WW3. The Blank Zone. Anne not existing. A world already broken before he arrived.

**Chapter 8** — Supply run setup. The bond between Som and his mother deepens.

## 13. THE TRILOGY

Another Sky is the **anchor story** — the longest, the most grounded, the most human.

| Story | Role | Year | Status |
