He woke up on the wrong side of four years. The same hands. The same phone. The same mother — except older, quieter, carrying grief he never watched her earn. The calendar said 2032. His body said 2028. Neither of them was lying.

Something was erased. Not destroyed — erased. Faces he loved no longer exist in this world. Photographs wiped. Names that produce only silence. The people who once made him feel like himself have been unmade, and the sky above the city is the wrong color, wrong shape, wrong in a way he cannot name but cannot stop seeing.

Then the outbreak began. What the news called a virus. What the bodies suggested was something older, worse, and entirely without mercy.

64% of the world will turn. Some of those who turn still think. Some still speak. Some will say your name in your dead mother's voice at 3 AM until you open the door.

He has not yet looked at what he lost. He is not ready. The sky won't wait.

████
